# Business Goals

The primary business goals of this analysis are:

1. Increase customer engagement and transaction activity.
2. Reduce fraudulent transactions and minimize financial losses.
3. Improve customer experience through better understanding of transaction behavior.
4. Identify high-value customers and support stronger customer retention.
5. Identify high-performing cities, states, and merchant categories for strategic growth.
6. Identify high-risk transaction patterns to support better fraud monitoring.
7. Improve understanding of customer spending behavior across age groups, locations, and transaction periods.
8. Identify opportunities for targeted marketing and personalized customer offers.
