# Business Problems

The analysis investigates the following business problems:

### Customer Spending

The business needs to understand how customers spend their money in order to improve customer engagement and transaction activity.

### Customer Activity

The business needs to understand when customers transact most frequently and when they spend the most.

### Customer Age

The business needs to understand how transaction behavior differs across age groups in order to support better customer segmentation.

### Merchant Category

The business needs to identify which merchant categories generate the highest transaction value and transaction frequency.

### Geographic Performance

The business needs to identify cities and states with high customer activity and high transaction value to support geographic growth strategies.

### Transaction Value

The business needs to identify high-value transactions and high-value customers to support retention and targeted customer strategies.

### Customer Value

The business needs to identify customers who contribute significantly to total transaction value.

### Fraud Risk

The business needs to understand fraud patterns across:

- Transaction hours
- Merchant categories
- Merchants
- States
- Cities
- Customer age groups
- Weekdays and weekends

Understanding these patterns can help improve fraud monitoring and reduce financial risk.
